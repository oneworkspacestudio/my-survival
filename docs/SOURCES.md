## Review boundaries

Instructions distinguish direct chemical exposure from nearby releases. Water
boiling is limited to microbial hazards and never recommended for chemical or
radioactive contamination. Refrigeration times are estimates, never automatic
safe-to-eat verdicts. The CPR card is labelled adult. First aid escalation is
explicit. No medicine doses, improvised explosives handling, tactical combat,
foraging identification, radiation detector or route-safety claims are included.

Before a broad public release, arrange qualified first-aid/content review, verify
source links again and update the review date only after that review. Version the
offline pack atomically; never replace an immutable published artifact.
